Off-the-shelf themes carry thousands of lines of unused JavaScript, bloated third-party plugin dependencies, and rigid visual containers that dilute luxury brand perception. On high-ticket transactions ($500–$5,000+), every 100ms delay in page delivery reduces conversion rates by up to 7%.

Our clean-sheet engineering guarantees 95+ Core Web Vitals, zero cumulative layout shifts (CLS), bespoke micro-interactions, and tailored checkout steps that eliminate cognitive friction, resulting in 20%–45% higher conversion efficiency.

Every optimization hypothesis is tethered to verifiable commercial attribution rather than superficial click vanity:

  • Cohort & Session Telemetry: Micro-friction mapping through heatmaps and event recording to identify exact drop-off triggers across checkout steps.
  • Controlled A/B Variant Testing: Statistically significant split-testing on headline framing, value propositions, and bundle logic to deliver sustainable AOV and CVR gains.
  • Blended ROAS & Retention Alignment: Direct synthesis between top-of-funnel acquisition economics and backend customer lifetime value (LTV).

Timelines are structured around rigorous milestone deliverables with zero operational downtime:
